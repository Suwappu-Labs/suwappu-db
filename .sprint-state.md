## Pre-existing semantics surfaced

The proof format needed redesign mid-implementation. First attempt
padded absence proofs with empty siblings to depth 20, but the verifier
then added phantom (step.byte, computed) entries to parent commitments,
producing roots that didn't match. Fixed by:

1. Variable-length proofs that terminate at the empty-child boundary.
2. `include_self_in_parent` flag in verify — `false` for the bottom
   step of an absence proof, `true` everywhere else.

Both inclusion and absence proofs now verify correctly across 10k cases.
